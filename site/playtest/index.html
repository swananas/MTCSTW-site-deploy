🌊 Watershed

A zen water & land sandbox. Raise and dig the terrain, place springs, and let running water do the building — it carves channels, carries silt, and lays down new land where it slows. Where the ground stays wet, green things move in on their own.

How demos land here: build on the Mac → export for web → drop the files in site/playtest/<name>/ in the mtcstw-site-deploy repo → push through the normal deploy gate. Serving cost is effectively $0 (static assets on the existing site Worker).
