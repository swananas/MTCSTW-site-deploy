TIERS & PRICING

Every pack is a monthly subscription, billed through Stripe. A single key can hold multiple packs. Current tiers:

  • Free — $0/mo: 1,000 calls/month across all data packs (sample data). Attribution required — credit MTCSTW visibly wherever the data appears.
  • Politics Pack — $69/mo: 50,000 calls/month. Campaign finance, lobbying, corporate data.
  • Labor Pack — $69/mo: 50,000 calls/month. Violations, OSHA, wage theft, Ghoul scores + brands.
  • PE Pack — $99/mo: 50,000 calls/month. Firms, portfolios, impacts, bankruptcy tracking.
  • Health Pack — $69/mo: 50,000 calls/month. Health-sector corporate data.
  • Patent Pack — $69/mo: 50,000 calls/month. Patent hoarders, evergreening, troll profiles.
  • All Access — $349/mo: 500,000 calls/month. Every data pack, plus webhooks and bulk downloads. Priority support.
  • Enterprise — $1,499/mo: unlimited calls, custom SLAs, dedicated support channel.

All paid packs include AI-detected connections. Prices are in USD and may change with 30 days’ notice — your current billing period always finishes at the price you paid.

QUOTAS & OVERAGE

Quotas are per calendar month and per pack. When you exceed your quota, the API returns HTTP 402 (Payment Required) until you upgrade your pack or the month resets. We don’t silently bill overage — you stay dark until you make a move.

Rate limits apply per key on top of monthly quotas. Abusing rate limits (bursting, rotating throwaway keys to dodge quotas, sharing keys across unrelated parties) is a Terms violation — see Termination.

ATTRIBUTION RULES

  • Free tier: visible attribution is mandatory — “Data via MTCSTW” with a link to mtcstw.com/api, wherever our data is displayed or published.
  • Paid tiers: attribution is appreciated but not required. (Keep it anyway. Solidarity.)
  • Don’t imply we endorse your product. Don’t strip attribution that was required when you were free.

WHAT’S PROHIBITED

Your keys die and your access ends if you:

  • Resell raw bulk data as a competing API — building a product on our data is fine; white-labeling our datasets into a competing data API is not.
  • Use the data for FCRA-prohibited purposes — no credit, employment, housing, or insurance eligibility decisions. Tenant screening, hiring screens, lending models, underwriting: hard no.
  • Attempt to re-identify anonymized individuals — donor identities are withheld from our datasets (see Terms, campaign-finance section). Cross-referencing or de-anonymizing is an instant, permanent ban.
  • Violate source terms of service — if you combine our data with other sources, you’re responsible for complying with those sources’ terms too. Don’t drag us into your ToS violations.
  • Scrape endpoints at abusive volume, share or sell keys, or probe private/internal endpoints.

DATA REALITY

Our datasets are aggregated from public sources (FEC filings, Senate lobbying records, OSHA data, and more) plus crowdsourced movement data. They’re powerful and they’re honest — but no dataset is complete or error-free. Verify before you publish or act on what you pull. What you build with the data is your responsibility.

KEYS & SECURITY

  • Keep your keys secret. Treat a key like a password — don’t commit it to public repos, don’t embed it in client-side code, don’t share it.
  • You’re responsible for traffic under your key. Rotate immediately if it leaks — you can rotate from your dashboard without contacting us.
  • We may rotate or revoke keys we believe are compromised, no warning needed.

No refunds for terminated-for-cause accounts.
