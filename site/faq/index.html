What is MTCSTW? What is The Propaganda Factory?

MTCSTW (Memes That Can Save The World) is the front. The Propaganda Factory is the engine. Together they’re a leftist media and organizing operation: real data about corporate power, an AI that shows receipts, and tools that turn outrage into action.

No think tank. No nonprofit board. A weapon.

How does Karl work? Where does his data come from?

Karl is our AI. You ask him a question — about a company, a CEO, a price, an eviction — and he answers with real data, not vibes. He pulls from 40+ query types across public records: corporate violations, CEO pay, political donations, prices, evictions, and more.

Every answer comes with receipts — the source, so you can verify it yourself. And when the data doesn’t have the answer, Karl says “I don’t know.” No hallucinating. No making it up.

What is the Ghoul Index? How is it scored?

The Ghoul Index ranks companies by misconduct. It pulls from 11 data sources: EPA violations, NLRB labor cases, CEO pay ratios, CFPB consumer complaints, FDA and CPSC recalls, SEC effective tax rates, lobbying spending, federal contracts, FEC political donations, and layoffs.

Each company gets a composite score weighted by severity and recency — a fresh wage-theft case counts more than a five-year-old paperwork fine. The worse the behavior, the higher the ghoul score.

What is Lux?

Lux is the bot that works while you sleep. It digs through the data looking for hidden connections — like “this company got federal contracts the same quarter it was fined for wage theft.”

When Lux finds something worth knowing, it feeds it to Karl and to the feed. Lux finds the story. Karl tells it. You spread it.

How do bounties work?

Bounties are missions. We post one — verify this price, photograph that shelf, dig up this record — and you complete it for XP. Some bounties are first-come, first-served. Some pay out to everyone who delivers.

Check the Bounties board, pick a mission, do the thing, get paid in XP.

What are photo bounties?

Photo bounties are simple: snap product prices in your area. Milk, eggs, rent signs, gas stations — whatever the bounty asks for.

Your photos build regional pricing data that no corporation will ever hand over. Aggregated, anonymized, and weaponized: “milk costs $X in Louisiana and the CEO made $Y.” Your camera is a data weapon.

How does XP work?

XP is how the movement measures contribution. You earn it for actions: votes, submissions, bounties, sharing, check-ins. More action, more XP, higher rank.

XP buys status, not stuff. There’s a daily cap so nobody games it, and real money is fully delinked — buying something never earns XP. You can’t buy your way up the board. You earn it.

What are first-claim bonuses and daily streaks?

First-claim pioneer bonuses: the first person to complete something new gets 2–3x XP. First to price-check a store. First to verify a bounty in a new zip code. Pioneers get paid.

Daily “first up” bonus: the first check-in of each day earns a bonus, and streaks multiply it. Show up every day and the machine notices.

Is my data private?

Yes — and we mean it structurally, not as a pinky promise. Your callsign is pseudonymous. We collect zip code, never GPS. We never require your real name. We never sell data. We run no ads and do no individual targeting or manipulation scoring.

Your activity becomes aggregated movement intelligence — “milk costs $X in Louisiana” — never a dossier on you. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

Is it free? How do you make money?

Free to use. No ads. We never sell your data. That’s not a marketing line, it’s the business model: the money comes from the movement itself — merch, subscriptions, War Bonds, creator revenue shares — never from advertisers, never from data brokers.

If a company can’t explain how it makes money without selling you, you’re the product. We can explain ours.

How do cells work?

Cells are local organizing groups. Same zip code, same fight. Find a cell near you, join one, or start your own. Cells get a dashboard — member lists, coordinated actions, leaderboards — tied into the feed so local wins get movement-wide attention.

The revolution is local. Cells are how it organizes.
