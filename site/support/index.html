Support

Questions about the Factory, your callsign, or your purchase? Start here.

What is a callsign? +

Your callsign is your identity across the entire Factory — every game, every cell, every medal. One name, everywhere. Claim it once and it's yours.

Do purchases give me XP? +

No. Real money = zero XP, always. War Bonds and subscriptions fund the movement — they don't buy advantages. XP is earned through action: missions, games, organizing.

How do I cancel my subscription? +

Every receipt email from Stripe includes a "manage subscription" link. Click it to cancel, change plans, or update your payment method. No questions, no retention gauntlet.

Where does my money go? +

Infrastructure, data feeds, tools — roughly $2,000/month to keep the Factory independent. We publish what we spend. No billionaire money, no ad revenue, no selling your data.

I lost my callsign / can't log in. +

Use the callsign recovery on the homepage — enter the email you registered with and we'll send a recovery link. If that doesn't work, contact us below.

Is my data sold? +

Never. Your activity powers the movement's intelligence (aggregated, never individual). We don't sell data, we don't run ads, we don't do surveillance capitalism.

Still stuck?

Reach out and a human will get back to you. For billing issues, include the email you used at checkout.
