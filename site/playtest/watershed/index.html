Try this: Raise a ridge across a stream and watch it pool, spill, and find a new way down. Dig a shallow channel and the river will move in, deepen it, and start building a delta where it flattens out. Grass holds the banks — seeded ground erodes much slower, and silt settles faster where it's green. Pale streaks on the water show which way the current is running.
